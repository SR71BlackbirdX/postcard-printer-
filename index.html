Message templates
Printing: the postcard prints at its exact size — 143 × 93 mm, landscape — with all app controls excluded.

Before your first print:
  1. Choose 100% / Actual size and turn off "Fit to page".
  2. Orientation landscape.
  3. Turn on background graphics (full-card mode only — text overlay prints no background).
  4. Front and Back produces two 143 × 93 mm pages; most printers need you to reinsert the card manually for the second side.

Browser scaling, paper size and printer margins all affect the physical result — verify with the calibration sheet before real postcards.
